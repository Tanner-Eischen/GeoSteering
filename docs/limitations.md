# Limitations

- The model is a single 2D synthetic cross-section with local feet-based coordinates. It does not establish geological realism for a field or support a 3D reservoir interpretation.
- Offsets provide sparse regional support. Their exact synthetic marker picks and a known layer-response calibration make this experiment more constrained than real interpretation.
- The GR response deliberately supplies signed center-distance information. Real GR curves need not identify which side of a target the bit occupies. Saturation removes precise center information; the replay carries forward unresolved interpretation and records that failure.
- The uncertainty band is assumed. It is not a calibrated confidence, prediction, or posterior interval.
- The optional ensemble is a separate conditional particle model with assumed spatial process noise, initial uncertainty and GR discrepancy. Its nominal 90% center intervals undercovered across the tested stresses (70.89% posterior coverage). Probabilities are not calibrated field confidence or whole-well success rates. Healthy effective sample size and no predictive-conflict flags can coexist with severe bias.
- Ensemble thickness remains an independent assumed prior because the signed-center GR model does not identify thickness. Center-only coverage does not validate top/base uncertainty or in-target probabilities. The model supports forward along-section observations and forecasts through 500 ft; it is not a general 3D earth-model posterior.
- Replay recommendations do not change the recorded path. Only causal commands change future simulated positions and observations.
- The causal response models inclination changes and geometric integration. It excludes toolface dynamics, bit/formation interaction, telemetry delay, drilling mechanics, pressure, well control, and operational safety controls.
- The landing build uses a 6 degrees/100 ft limit; causal lateral comparisons use 3 degrees/100 ft. Mixing these intervals would misstate constraint compliance.
- The historical reference uses a single seed and geological scenario. The separate robustness study adds eight predefined evaluation configurations and five paired noise seeds, with a disclosed validity wrapper around the unchanged controller. These handpicked cases provide descriptive robustness evidence, not population or operational performance estimates. Cases and outcomes are now observed and cannot be reused as an untouched holdout for future tuning. No target-success threshold is used to tune a run after evaluation.
- Several robustness configurations change geology and sensor stress together; the study is not a factorial attribution of individual effects. Exact synthetic offset picks and a signed-distance sensor remain favorable assumptions, even when its calibration is biased or measurements are censored/delayed.
- A command preview projects one geometric station under captured limits; it is not a geological forecast or a safety assessment. Legacy causal runs without a public mechanics snapshot require a new run to enable the preview.
- Fork comparisons describe complete recorded branch histories. Multiple commands and interpretation revisions can differ after the shared prefix, so an outcome difference must not be attributed to one isolated command without a separately controlled experiment. The shared prefix is included in the reported lateral denominator.
- Numeric scoring approximates the nonlinear truth field at a documented integration resolution. Estimated piecewise-linear zone scores use exact interval integration.
- Project storage is local and intended for a single writer. There is no multi-user database, operational deployment, or production certification.
- Older projects without spatial references can be inspected, but cannot establish common-datum interpretation or steering performance until reimported with explicit references.
- Browser verification is limited to the recorded local Windows/Chrome desktop, half-width and narrow-viewport profile in `reports/browser-qa-2026-11-01/`. It does not establish other browsers/devices, multi-user behavior, deployment readiness or operational drilling fitness. Earlier packets retain their historical unverified-UI status.
